# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of identity,
experience, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, respect privacy, accept technical disagreement, and focus criticism on observable
work. Do not publish another person's private data or use project channels for harassment.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the repository's security or
maintainer contact channel. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this
policy.
